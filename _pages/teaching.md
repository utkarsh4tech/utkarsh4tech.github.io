# My Teaching Experience

Over the past 20+ months, I've had the immense pleasure of serving as a Teaching Assistant across diverse and dynamic learning environments—from the rigorous academic programs at [`IIT Madras`](https://study.iitm.ac.in/ds/academics.html#AC16) and [`IIT Ropar`](https://masaischool.com/iit-ropar-ai-cse) to the industry-focused bootcamps at [`AccioJobs`](https://acciojob.com/data-science-and-ai).

# My Teaching Philosophy

I am a strong advocate for a hands-on, `"theory-to-practice"` approach. My teaching style focuses on building a strong intuition for core concepts before diving into the mathematical rigor. I strive to create a supportive and interactive environment where students feel comfortable asking questions, making mistakes, and learning collaboratively. Ultimately, my aim is not just to teach tools and techniques, but **to foster the critical thinking and problem-solving skills necessary to excel in the real world.**
